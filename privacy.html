Privacy Policy for Jewellogic

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Jewellogic ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Jewellogic (the "Application").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Provided directly by You

  • Business & Profile Information: Firm Name, Shop Address, City, and State (entered during billing template configuration or account setup).
  • Contact Information: Mobile Phone Number used for authentication, account identification, and service communication.

B. Automatically Collected Data

  • Transaction & Calculation Data: Metal rates (Gold/Silver), weight calculations, 18K/20K purities, making charges, and GST calculations. All calculation data is stored locally on your device via secure local storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

We utilize trusted third-party service providers to facilitate app operations, process subscriptions, and display advertisements:

  • RevenueCat: Used for managing in-app purchases and subscription statuses. RevenueCat collects anonymous purchase histories and app user IDs.
    Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

  • Google AdMob / Google Ads: Used to display advertisements within the Application. Google may use Advertising IDs (GAID) and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on user settings.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate accurate gold/silver and jewelry calculations.
  • To generate personalized invoices/bills featuring your firm name and address.
  • To process subscription access and feature entitlements via RevenueCat.
  • To display relevant ads via Google AdMob.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and optimize app performance.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage First: Your trade calculations, rate settings, and invoice records remain strictly on your local device. We do not store your private trade calculations on central servers.
  • Security: We employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information and mobile credentials.

5. Data Deletion Rights (Account Deletion)

Under Google Play policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

You can request account deletion directly within the Application settings or by emailing us at ighanshyamsaraf@gmail.com.

Upon request, your mobile number and profile details stored on our authentication servers will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Application is intended for business use by individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
